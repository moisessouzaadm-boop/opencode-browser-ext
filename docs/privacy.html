Privacy Policy — OpenCode Browser

We collect nothing. The OpenCode Browser extension:

  • Does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data, browsing history, credentials, or page content to any third party.
  • All communication stays on your machine (localhost WebSocket to the local MCP server, Chrome tabs API).
  • Stores only functional settings locally via chrome.storage.local (MCP ports, parked-port cache). No analytics, no trackers, no remote requests.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/moisessouzaadm-boop/opencode-browser-ext.
